About Me

I am more than happy to be contacted on LinkedIn or by email.

I am from Wrexham, North Wales. I have Cerebral Palsy which affects my speech and movement; I use both powered and manual wheelchairs to move around and a DynaVox (Talking communication aid) to talk. However, I don’t let it bring me down and I don’t see it as a barrier to stopping me from doing what I am passionate about, which is game development.

I recently received the Owain Glyndwr Society Prize for Alumni due to my dedication during my time as an undergraduate student at Wrexham University between October 2020 to May 2023. I am very appreciative of receiving this award because there are thousands of students every year so the chances of being chosen are very small.
